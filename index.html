The idea

Move from paying for reach to paying for members

The Club is an experience product. Its value is hard to believe from an ad and easy to believe when someone you follow actually uses it.

The buyer trusts peers

Club members are founders, investors and senior professionals. They are time-poor, buy on value, and listen to people like them more than to brands.

Use is the proof

Private clubs, golf, hotel upgrades and lounges make sense the moment someone shows them. A year of Orange is worth ₹3 lakh on paper; a creator shows it in real life.

Spend follows sales

Most creator cost is commission on memberships sold, so the programme costs more only when it is working.

Proposed targets, to confirm with DreamFolks: 15 Insiders live in month 1 and 50 by month 4; a clear, tracked share of Orange and Black sales coming through creator codes; cost per member from creators at or below Meta by month 3.

Selection rules

  • Mostly 10,000 to 100,000 followers; larger names only as Hosts
  • Audience mainly in the metros and aged 25 to 45, checked from their insights
  • Past sponsored posts that people saved and shared, not just liked
  • Lives in or travels to cities with good Club coverage
  • Willing to show real use and give honest opinions

Who we avoid

  • Deal and coupon pages: they attract discount hunters, not Club buyers
  • Accounts with bought followers
  • Audiences mostly under 25

How it works

Three levels, and each step up is earned

The base is open to every member. Creators move up on memberships sold and on posts that perform as ads, never on follower count alone.

By invitation

Hosts

The top 5 to 8 Insiders. Black membership and a monthly fee. They host small member experiences: a golf day, a club evening.

Top sellers whose posts work as ads

The core

Insiders

15 creators at launch, growing to 50. A complimentary Orange membership for 3 posts in 60 days, plus commission on every membership their code sells.

Members who post well can apply

Open to all

Members

Every existing member gets a personal code to share. An extra benefit for the member, and a welcome perk for the friend who joins.

How a creator joins

  1. Wirality shortlists creators against the selection rules, and DreamFolks approves the list.
  2. The creator signs simple terms: posting, disclosure, usage rights and payout details.
  3. Their Orange membership is activated and they get a personal code and link.
  4. They post 3 times in 60 days from the monthly brief. Each post is checked before it counts.
  5. At day 60 we review sales and content results: renew, promote to Host, or part ways.

Payout rules

  • Paid monthly by bank transfer or UPI, after the Club’s refund window closes
  • No commission on a creator’s own purchase, or when a second discount code is used
  • Codes found on coupon sites are switched off
  • Tax deducted at source before payout, with a monthly statement
  • Minimum payout of ₹500; smaller balances roll over

Content

One brief a month, five formats built for the Club

Creators shouldn’t need a new idea every time. Each picks a benefit and a format from the monthly brief.

What the monthly brief carries

  • The month’s message pillar (Play, Travel or Everyday) and 2 to 3 benefits to feature
  • Five formats, each with three example opening lines
  • What to show, what not to claim, and the exact disclosure line
  • Partner venues to visit that month, with booking help from DreamFolks

The quality check

Every post is checked before it counts. Within two working days a Wirality reviewer confirms the benefit is shown accurately, the disclosure is right, and nothing is promised that the Club doesn’t include, especially domestic airport lounges. Approved posts go into a shared library, marked with how we may use them.

Turning posts into ads

The organic posts are the test; the winners become ads

This is where most of the programme’s value to DreamFolks comes from: a steady supply of real-use creative, tested before paid money goes in.

  1. Weekly pull. Rank the week’s approved posts by saves, shares, watch time and code sales.
  2. Shortlist. Take the top few whose terms allow paid use.
  3. Run them as partnership ads on Instagram from the creator’s own handle, aimed at Orange.
  4. Reward the winners. Creators whose ad beats the target cost per member get the ad bonus and move toward Host.
  5. Feed back. Winning opening lines and formats go into next month’s brief, so every Insider copies what sells.

Doing it right in India

Every Insider post is an ad, and is treated as one

A free membership and commission are both a relationship with the brand. Each rule is built into the terms, the brief, the quality check and the payout.
